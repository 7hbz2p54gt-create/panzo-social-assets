MEDIUM (~150 words)
Interior and fit-out firms usually run on WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets and a notebook on site. Panzo replaces that with one connected platform, from the first enquiry to handover and after-sales service.

Every lead gets an owner and a follow-up date. Deals turn into room-wise BOQs and quotations clients can approve. Approved work flows into procurement, purchase orders and site execution, where the team files daily site reports from the phone, even offline. Billing is tax-correct for India GST, UAE VAT and Singapore GST, and every payment is tied to its project and milestone. Handover and service tickets stay on the same project record.

Built-in Panzo AI drafts, reviews and explains quotations. Panzo runs on web, iOS and Android, and is built for interior, fit-out, modular and turnkey firms in India, the UAE and Singapore.
